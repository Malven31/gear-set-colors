# Gear Set Colors

<!-- Download badge for CurseForge project 1814234, served by shields.io. -->
![Downloads](https://img.shields.io/curseforge/dt/1814234?logo=curseforge&label=downloads)

**Never sell a piece of your gear by mistake again.** Gear Set Colors marks every item in your bags that belongs to one of your equipment sets with that set's colour: your tank gear in one colour, your healing gear in another, your fishing outfit in a third. One look at your bags before you sell, and you know what to keep.

Made for **World of Warcraft: Forever**.

## What it does

- **Marks set items in your bags** with their set's colour. Choose how, in any combination:
  - a coloured border around the slot (on by default);
  - a light coloured tint over the icon (on by default);
  - a small square per set in the slot's corner.
- **One colour per set, picked for you:** red, cyan, yellow, pink, orange, lime, white, brown. Green, blue and purple are left out, so the marks never look like item quality borders. Change any of them with the colour picker, or stop marking a set.
- **Items in several sets** show every set's colour: the border alternates between them, and the corner squares show one per set.
- **Shared by your characters:** colours go by set name, so a "Tank" set has the same colour on every character.
- **In the Equipment Manager**, a colour square on each set's icon: click it to change the colour.
- **Exact:** items are recognised by their place in your bags, as the Equipment Manager records them, so two identical rings in two different sets each get the right colour.
- **Always up to date:** the marks follow your bags as items move, as you equip gear, and as you save or change sets.

## Quick start

1. Save your equipment sets in the Equipment Manager (Character window), if you haven't yet.
2. Open your bags: each set's items are framed and tinted in the set's colour.
3. Type **/gsc** to change colours or how items are marked.

The options are also under Options > AddOns > Gear Set Colors, and in the addon compartment.

## Good to know

- **Blizzard's bags only**, one window per bag or all bags combined. Bag replacement addons (Bagnon, Baganator, ArkInventory...) draw their own bag windows, which aren't marked.
- **The bank isn't marked.**
